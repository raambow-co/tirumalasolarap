Frequently Asked Questions

Essential facts regarding PM Surya Ghar grants, net-metering protocols with APEPDCL and APSPDCL, and rooftop longevity.

For residential homes in Andhra Pradesh, the Central Government provides:
• 1 kW system: ₹30,000 direct subsidy.
• 2 kW system: ₹60,000 direct subsidy.
• 3 kW to 10 kW systems: Fixed ₹78,000 maximum direct DBT grant.
Funds are deposited directly into your bank account after APEPDCL or APSPDCL inspects your rooftop and installs your bi-directional net meter.
Your existing electricity meter is replaced with a bi-directional smart net meter. During peak daytime sun, when your panels produce surplus power that your household doesn't consume, it automatically flows back into the state electricity grid. You receive full credits on your bill, paying only for the net difference at the end of each billing cycle.
Each 1 kW requires around 80 to 100 square feet of shadow-free rooftop area:
• 3 kW System: Approximately 250 to 300 Sq. Ft.
• 5 kW System: Approximately 400 to 500 Sq. Ft.
If your rooftop has overhead tanks or parapets, we can construct elevated superstructures (7 to 9 feet high) so you retain complete usable terrace space beneath.
We exclusively use MNRE-certified Tier-1 Mono PERC and Bifacial panels carrying a 25-Year Performance Guarantee. Grid inverters are protected with 5 to 10-year replacement warranties, and all mounting structures are hot-dip galvanized with anti-corrosion coating engineered to resist coastal moisture.
Yes, entirely! Our liaison specialists take care of every procedural step:
1. National Portal application at pmsuryaghar.gov.in.
2. Feasibility sanction from APEPDCL / APSPDCL.
3. Site readiness, test certificates, and Discom inspection.
4. Net meter procurement and grid synchronization.
5. Commissioning verification and subsidy release tracking.
